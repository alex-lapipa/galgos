# Organizational & Institutional Mapping of the Galgo Español (Spain) — Knowledge-Base Batch

## TL;DR
- Spain's galgo world splits into two opposed institutional blocs: a **hunting/coursing establishment** (Federación Española de Galgos + Real Federación Española de Caza, which states on its own site that "más de 350.000 personas" form part of it, while an independent Deloitte study for Fundación Artemisan counted 330,423 federated sport-hunters and 891,889 valid autonomous hunting licences as of 31 Dec 2023) and a large **animal-welfare/rescue network** (hundreds of protectoras plus international adoption partners), mediated by a **cynological establishment** (RSCE / Club Nacional del Galgo Español) and, since 2020, a **government animal-rights administration** (Dirección General de Derechos de los Animales).
- The defining recent conflict is the **hunting-dog exemption in Ley 7/2023** (Ley de Bienestar Animal): welfare groups (SOS Galgos, Plataforma NAC, PACMA, CAS Int'l, AnimaNaturalis) fought to include hunting dogs; the hunting lobby (RFEC) secured their exclusion.
- The widely repeated "50,000–100,000 galgos abandoned per year" figure is an **activist estimate without official census backing** — Newtral (26 Oct 2021) confirms "En España no existe una estadística oficial sobre el abandono de animales," and the range traces to the "Yo Galgo" crowdfunding campaign ("Cada año, entre 50.000 y 100.000 galgos españoles son asesinados o abandonados," lamarea.com, 3 Mar 2016); it should be tagged CONTESTED.

## Key Findings
- The galgo institutional landscape spans at least 15 organization typologies. Rescue is dominated by a few large hubs (Scooby, Fundación Benjamín Mehnert, Galgos del Sol, SOS Galgos) plus a long tail of small provincial protectoras, heavily dependent on Northern-European and US adoption/funding.
- The hunting side is formally organized as a **sport**: the Federación Española de Galgos (FEG, constituted 1939) runs the annual Campeonato de España de Galgos en Campo (Copa de S.M. El Rey), currently in its LXXXVIII (88th) edition, with the 2026 final scheduled at Cuesta de los Picos, Nava del Rey (Valladolid), on 24 January 2026 (per fedegalgos.com).
- Abandonment figures are genuinely contested and should be tagged CONTESTED in the knowledge graph. SEPRONA numbers are far lower than activist figures — official SEPRONA data cited by dudoteca.com refer to "un máximo de 50 galgos abandonados al año, hubo un año que fueron 3," while PACMA's own protectora survey "publicaron... que 5.588 galgos fueron abandonados en 2019." Fundación Affinity publishes the most methodologically-cited abandonment study.
- International rescue networks (Germany, Netherlands, UK, USA, Belgium) are structurally essential — Spanish shelters state they could not operate without foreign adoptions and donations.

## Details — Organization Entries by Batch

### BATCH A — RESCUE_SHELTER (Spain)

**A1. SOS Galgos**
- Type: RESCUE_SHELTER; legal form: asociación sin ánimo de lucro
- Founded: 2000 (some sources 1999), Barcelona, following closure of the Barcelona canódromo
- Founders: Anna Clements (British; Director General) and Albert Sordé (Catalan vet; Director Ejecutivo, Tres Vet clinic); a married couple
- HQ: Esplugues de Llobregat, Barcelona (Cataluña); scope national
- Mission/activities: rescue, adoption, legal/educational campaigning; ~350 galgos rehomed per year
- Stance: ANTI_HUNTING (goal: abolition of hunting with galgos)
- Key people: Anna Clements, Albert Sordé, Almudena Romero (VP honor, Cádiz)
- Web: sosgalgos.org; @SOSGalgos
- Notable: 2024 campaign "Nos cag*mos en la ley 7/2023" against the hunting-dog exemption

**A2. Protectora y Santuario Scooby (Scooby Medina)**
- Type: RESCUE_SHELTER; legal form: Sociedad Protectora de Animales y Medioambiente
- Founded: 1987, Medina del Campo (Valladolid), by a group of women; internationalized mid-1990s under president Fermín Pérez
- HQ: Medina del Campo (Valladolid, Castilla y León); refuges also in Zamora and Palencia; scope national/international
- Mission: largest shelter in Spain; ~600 galgos rescued/year; ~80% of dogs come from hunting; 15+ transport trips to Europe/year
- Stance: ANTI_HUNTING / welfare
- Key people: Fermín Pérez (president)
- Web: scoobymedina.org

**A3. Fundación Benjamín Mehnert (FBM)**
- Type: RESCUE_SHELTER / FUNDER_FOUNDATION; legal form: fundación, declared Entidad de Interés Público
- Founded: 2000 by Gisela Mehnert (German émigré); galgo focus intensified 2008
- HQ: Alcalá de Guadaíra (Sevilla, Andalucía), 20,000 m² recovery centre; scope national/international
- Mission: rescue/recovery/rehoming of galgos and podencos; ~1,000+ adoptions/year; ~600 animals in care
- Stance: WELFARE / ANTI-abandonment
- Key people: Gisela Mehnert (founder), Rocío Arrabal (vet/director), Isabel Paiva (directora)
- Partners: Germany, Belgium, France, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Italy, Finland, USA, Canada, UK (Project Galgo)
- Web: fundacionbm.com; @fundacionbenjaminmehnert

**A4. Galgos del Sol (GDS)**
- Type: RESCUE_SHELTER; registered charity in Spain, UK, and USA (501(c)(3))
- Founded: 2010, Murcia, by Tina Solera (British expat, moved to Spain 2007)
- HQ: Murcia (Región de Murcia); purpose-built Rescue & Education Centre; scope regional/international
- Mission: rescue/rehab/rehome galgos+podencos; 250+ dogs in care; education programs in schools
- Stance: WELFARE (Solera emphasizes distinguishing abusers from galgueros in some coverage)
- Key people: Tina Solera (founder/president)
- Partners: Greyhounds in Need UK (early funder), adoptions to UK/US/Belgium
- Web: galgosdelsol.org

**A5. Galgos del Sur**
- Type: RESCUE_SHELTER; asociación sin ánimo de lucro; CIF G14894323
- Founded: 2010, Córdoba
- HQ: Córdoba (Andalucía); ~120–131 dogs in care; scope provincial
- Mission: rescue of galgos/hunting dogs; 400+ rescued in a peak year; 25+ legal cases against abusers
- Stance: ANTI_HUNTING (president Javier Luna linked to PACMA; hunting press critical)
- Key people: Javier Luna (president)
- Web: galgosdelsur.es
- Note: no public subsidies (self-reported); receives criticism from Club-Caza (hunting media) — flag as contested/partisan source

**A6. Galgos 112 (Associació Protectora de Llebrers Galgos 112)**
- Type: RESCUE_SHELTER; asociación registered in Cataluña
- Founded: 13 April 2008, Sant Feliu de Guíxols (Girona)
- HQ: Girona (Cataluña); delegations across Spain + Switzerland; collaborates NL/BE/FR; scope national/international
- Mission: recognition of lebreles/galgos/podencos as companion animals; legislative + educational work
- Stance: ANTI_HUNTING / welfare
- Web: galgos112.es
- Notable: private prosecution (with INTERcids lawyers) → 1-yr prison sentence, Jaén 2024

**A7. 112 Carlota Galgos**

**A8. Salvando Peludos**

### BATCH C — HUNTING_FEDERATION & COURSING_CLUB

**C1. Federación Española de Galgos (FEG)**
- Type: HUNTING_FEDERATION (federación deportiva)
- Lineage: first national body "Club Deportivo Español" (1929–1932); current FEG constituted 1939
- Scope: national; regulates carreras de galgos en campo (open-field coursing after live hare) + historically canódromo & liebre mecánica
- Key event: Campeonato de España de Galgos en Campo — Copa de S.M. El Rey (annual; LXXXVIII/88th edition 2025–26, final at Nava del Rey 24 Jan 2026)
- Stance: PRO_HUNTING
- Key people: Luis Ángel Vegas (president)
- Web: fedegalgos.com
- Regional bodies: Federación Andaluza de Galgos, Federación de Castilla y León de Galgos (pres. David Jiménez), Federación Extremeña de Galgos, and others

**C2. Real Federación Española de Caza (RFEC)**
- Type: HUNTING_FEDERATION; federación deportiva
- Scope: national; RFEC states "más de 350.000 personas" form part of it; the Deloitte study for Fundación Artemisan (Madrid, 6 May 2025) counted 330,423 federated sport-hunters and 891,889 valid autonomous licences plus 17,624 inter-autonomous licences (31 Dec 2023); hunting ranks 3rd sport by licences after football and basketball
- Activities: lobbying (via FACE at EU level), Fedenca (research foundation), Escuela Española de Caza
- Stance: PRO_HUNTING; secured hunting-dog exemption in Ley 7/2023
- Key people: Manuel Gallardo Casado (president)
- Web: fecaza.com
- Controversy: published a video projecting a telescopic sight over the face of the DG Derechos de los Animales, which was judicialized

**C3. Historic coursing societies (CULTURAL/COURSING lineage):** Sociedad Coto La Ina (Jerez, 1910; founder Juan Pedro Domecq); Sociedad El Goloso (Madrid, 1913); Copa Algete (from 1915). Historic canódromos (Madrid/Carabanchel, Barcelona) — Alfonso XIII inaugurated liebre-mecánica racing at the Stadium Metropolitano in April 1930. 28 canódromos once existed; track racing effectively extinct; Campeonato de España en pista ran 1931–2005.

### BATCH H — VETERINARY_SCIENTIFIC & ACADEMIC

**H1. Consejo General de Colegios Veterinarios de España (OCV/CGCVE)** — VETERINARY_SCIENTIFIC; HQ Villanueva 11, Madrid; president Gonzalo Moreno. Official position on Ley de Bienestar Animal; proposed "Perito Forense Veterinario." Stance: REGULATORY.

**H2. Universidad de Córdoba – GISAZ (Grupo de Investigación en Sanidad Animal y Zoonosis)** — ACADEMIC; leishmaniasis research in hunting zones (Castro del Río study 2022–23; dog prevalence 73.2%), relevant to galgo/podenco health. Also UAB — Laia Solano-Gallego (canine leishmaniasis).

**H3. Fundación Affinity (Observatorio / Estudio "Él nunca lo haría")** — VETERINARY_SCIENTIFIC/FUNDER; publishes Spain's standard abandonment/adoption study since 2013.
